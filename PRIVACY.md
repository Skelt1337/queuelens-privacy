# QueueLens Privacy Policy

_Effective October 24, 2026_

QueueLens does not collect, sell, or share your data. It has no servers of its own, and no analytics, tracking, or advertising service ever receives your data. The only server QueueLens talks to is your organization's own TeamDynamix server.

**What it accesses.** Using your own sign-in, QueueLens reads and — only when you click — updates tickets in your organization's TeamDynamix system. It can do only what your TeamDynamix account is already allowed to do.

**What it stores, and where.** Your sign-in token and a cached copy of your queue are kept in browser memory only. They're cleared when you sign out, close the browser, or reload the extension. Your settings, reply templates, and reminders (a ticket number and a due date) are stored locally in your browser and are never synced.

**What it never stores.** Requestor contact details, ticket descriptions, and pasted email text are kept only in memory while you use them, never written to disk, and never sent anywhere except your organization's TeamDynamix server.
